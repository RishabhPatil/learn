    CORE IDEAS & RESEARCH DIRECTIONS

    Follow the ideas back to their sources.

    Start with nine core chapters, including M-RoPE, then explore MRoPE-I, MHRoPE, Circle-RoPE, and VRoPE in Research directions. These works address different design questions; their reading order is not a historical sequence of replacements. Other approaches remain available for focused comparisons. Every explorer links to a primary paper or implementation. The configurations deliberately share a small context so you can separate changes to coordinates, frequencies, and positional biases. They are not benchmark reproductions.
