    A LIVING MAP OF THE LITERATURE

    Follow the ideas back to their sources.

    Every explorer above links to a primary paper or implementation. The configurations deliberately share a small context so you can separate changes to coordinates, frequencies, and positional biases. They are not benchmark reproductions.

    Further research · not yet implemented in this lab

    These entries are a research queue, not interactive implementations. A method is added to the explorer only after its construction and a testable example are checked. This atlas does not claim to cover all published positional-encoding research.

    01

    Pixels → patches → tokens

    A patch contains many pixels. Vision encoders may merge patches further before the language model. The examples use a fixed 4 × 4 patch grid to make the indexing visible. This is not a checkpoint’s tokenizer.

    Vision Transformer paper ↗
    02

    Coordinates ≠ embeddings

    A three-tuple supplies positional indices to many rotation pairs. This scene plots those indices. It is not a 3D projection of a trained model’s hidden states.

    Qwen2-VL paper ↗
    03

    Indices ≠ phases

    Methods can share the same position indices and differ in how those indices drive frequencies. Compare the coordinate scene with the frequency allocation and component views.

    RoFormer paper ↗
