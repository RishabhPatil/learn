A LIVING MAP OF THE LITERATURE

Follow the ideas back to their sources.

Every explorer above links to a primary paper or implementation. The configurations deliberately share a small context so you can separate changes to coordinates, frequencies, and attention biases. They are not benchmark reproductions.

Further research · not yet implemented in this lab

These entries are a research queue, not interactive implementations. A method is added to the explorer only after its construction and a testable example are checked. This atlas does not claim to cover all published positional-encoding research.

01

Pixels → patches → tokens

A patch contains many pixels. Vision encoders may merge patches further before the language model. Changing this lab’s grid changes the illustrative tokenization; it does not change an actual checkpoint.

Vision Transformer paper ↗
02

Coordinates ≠ embeddings

A three-tuple supplies positional indices to many rotation pairs. This scene plots those indices. It is not a 3D projection of a trained model’s hidden states.

Qwen2-VL paper ↗
03

Geometry ≠ learned attention

Attention also depends on learned content features, layer, head, mask, and scale. The lab exposes its Q/K assumptions so a visually compelling pattern cannot be mistaken for a model’s explanation.
